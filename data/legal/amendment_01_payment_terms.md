# Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") to the Master Services Agreement dated January 15, 2025 (the "Agreement") is entered into as of June 1, 2025 by and between Northwind Analytics Ltd. ("Provider") and Blue Harbor Retail Group Inc. ("Client").

## 1. Background

The Parties wish to amend Section 3 (Fees and Payment) of the Agreement to reflect Client's revised accounts-payable cycle.

## 2. Amendment of Section 3 (Fees and Payment)

Section 3 of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

"Client shall pay the fees set forth in each applicable SOW. Provider shall invoice Client monthly in arrears. Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days of receipt (net 45). Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. Client shall notify Provider of any disputed amount within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the invoice."

## 3. No Other Changes

Except as expressly set forth in this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect and is unchanged. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.

## 4. Governing Law

This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, consistent with Section 10 of the Agreement.
