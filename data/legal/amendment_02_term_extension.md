# Amendment No. 2 to Master Services Agreement

This Amendment No. 2 ("Amendment") to the Master Services Agreement dated January 15, 2025, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated June 1, 2025 (the "Agreement"), is entered into as of July 10, 2026 by and between Northwind Analytics Ltd. ("Provider") and Blue Harbor Retail Group Inc. ("Client").

## 1. Background

The Parties wish to extend the term of the Agreement beyond its original expiration date.

## 2. Extension of Term

Section 2 (Term) of the Agreement is hereby amended by deleting the phrase "expiring on January 14, 2027" and replacing it with the phrase "expiring on January 14, 2029." As amended, the initial term of the Agreement expires on January 14, 2029.

## 3. No Other Changes

Except as expressly set forth in this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect and is unchanged. In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment controls.

## 4. Governing Law

This Amendment is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, consistent with Section 10 of the Agreement.
