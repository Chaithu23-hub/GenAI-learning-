# Data Processing and Consulting Agreement — DPCA-2024-0018

**Document type:** Contract with amendments | **Effective date:** 2024-02-15
**Corpus note:** Sample for RAG demonstration only. Not an executed instrument.

## Parties
- **Client:** FinTrust Capital Advisors Pvt. Ltd., Level 12, Prestige Towers, Nariman Point,
  Mumbai, Maharashtra 400 021.
- **Consultant:** DataBridge Analytics LLP, Unit 304, Salarpuria Tech Park, Marathahalli,
  Bengaluru, Karnataka 560 037.

---

## Section 1 — Services

**1.1 Consulting Services.** Consultant shall provide data analytics consulting, model
development, and reporting services as described in each Project Brief executed by both Parties.

**1.2 Project Briefs.** Each Project Brief sets out the deliverables, timeline, fees, and
acceptance criteria for a specific engagement. Project Briefs are incorporated by reference.

**1.3 Standard of Care.** Consultant shall perform all services professionally, by qualified
personnel, consistent with industry standards for financial data analytics consulting.

**1.4 Tools and Methodology.** Consultant retains the right to choose the methodology, tools,
and frameworks used to deliver the services, subject to Client's security and compliance policies.

---

## Section 2 — Term

**2.1 Initial Term.** 2024-02-15 to 2026-02-14 (two years), unless earlier terminated.

**2.2 Renewal.** Automatically renews for one-year periods unless either Party gives sixty
(60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Term.

---

## Section 3 — Fees and Payment

**3.1 Fee Basis.** Fees are time-and-materials at the rates in each Project Brief, unless
a Project Brief specifies a fixed fee for a defined scope.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Consultant invoices monthly for time-and-materials work or at milestones
for fixed-fee work.

**3.3 Payment Terms.** Client shall pay each invoice within twenty-one (21) calendar days
of the invoice date.

**3.4 Late Payment Interest.** Undisputed overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month
(or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower) from the due date until
the date of actual payment.

**3.5 Expenses.** Pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses (travel, accommodation) are reimbursed
at cost with receipts. Expense claims must be submitted within thirty (30) days of incurring
the expense.

**3.6 Rate Adjustments.** Consultant may revise time-and-materials rates at each Renewal Term
on forty-five (45) days' written notice. Increases shall not exceed seven percent (7%) per year.

---

## Section 4 — Intellectual Property

**4.1 Client-Owned Deliverables.** Upon full payment, all reports, models, code, and
documentation created specifically for Client under a Project Brief become Client's exclusive
property. Consultant assigns all associated IP rights.

**4.2 Consultant Background IP.** Consultant retains all pre-existing tools, frameworks,
methodologies, and libraries. Consultant grants Client a non-exclusive licence to use embedded
Background IP solely to exploit the Deliverables for Client's internal purposes.

**4.3 Residual Knowledge.** Nothing prevents Consultant from using generalised knowledge,
skills, and experience retained in personnel memory for future engagements, provided no
Client Confidential Information is disclosed.

---

## Section 5 — Confidentiality

**5.1 Obligations.** Each Party keeps the other's Confidential Information strictly confidential,
uses it solely for this Agreement, and does not disclose it to third parties without consent.

**5.2 Financial Data.** All Client financial data, portfolio information, and client lists
are deemed Confidential Information regardless of labelling.

**5.3 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination for six (6) years. Trade
secrets are protected indefinitely.

**5.4 Return or Destruction.** On termination or request, the Receiving Party returns or
destroys all Confidential Information within ten (10) business days and certifies in writing.

---

## Section 6 — Data Protection and Security

**6.1 Personal Data Processing.** Consultant processes any Personal Data within Client datasets
only on Client's instructions and solely to deliver the agreed services.

**6.2 Security Measures.** Consultant shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and
organisational security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls,
and annual security assessments.

**6.3 Breach Notification.** Consultant notifies Client within twenty-four (24) hours of
discovering any security incident or personal data breach affecting Client data.

**6.4 Sub-processors.** Consultant shall not engage sub-processors to process Client Personal
Data without Client's prior written approval.

**6.5 Data Deletion.** On termination, Consultant deletes all Client data and Personal Data
within fifteen (15) business days and provides written certification.

---

## Section 7 — Representations and Warranties

**7.1 Consultant Warranties.** Consultant warrants that:
(a) deliverables will materially conform to Project Brief specifications;
(b) services will be performed by personnel with appropriate qualifications; and
(c) services and deliverables will not infringe third-party IP Rights to Consultant's knowledge.

**7.2 Client Warranties.** Client warrants that all data provided to Consultant is lawfully
obtained and that Client has authority to share it with Consultant for the agreed purpose.

---

## Section 8 — Limitation of Liability

**8.1 No Indirect Damages.** Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive
damages including lost profits, business opportunity, or data loss.

**8.2 Aggregate Cap.** Total liability of either Party for all claims shall not exceed the
total fees paid by Client to Consultant in the six (6) months before the event giving rise to
the claim.

**8.3 Exceptions.** The cap does not limit: liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross
negligence; Client's payment obligations; Consultant's indemnity obligations; or liability for
breach of confidentiality or data protection obligations.

---

## Section 9 — Indemnification

**9.1 Consultant.** Consultant indemnifies Client against third-party IP infringement claims
arising from the Deliverables used in accordance with this Agreement.

**9.2 Client.** Client indemnifies Consultant against third-party claims arising from
Client data infringing third-party rights or from Client's breach of this Agreement.

---

## Section 10 — Termination

**10.1 For Convenience.** Either Party may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice.
Consultant is paid for work completed and non-cancellable expenses incurred to the notice date.

**10.2 For Cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other
materially breaches and fails to cure within twenty-one (21) days of written notice, or
becomes insolvent.

**10.3 No Further Obligation.** On termination, no further fees are due beyond amounts owed
for work already performed and accepted; pre-paid unused fixed fees are refunded pro-rata.

---

## Section 11 — General

**11.1 Governing Law.** Laws of India. Arbitration seat: Mumbai, Maharashtra.

**11.2 Dispute Resolution.** Senior management negotiation for thirty (30) days, then binding
arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 (India), English language.

**11.3 Notices.** Written, by registered post or email with read-receipt confirmation.

**11.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and all Project Briefs constitute the entire agreement.

**11.5 Force Majeure.** No liability for delays beyond reasonable control. If the event
continues beyond thirty (30) days, either Party may terminate on fourteen (14) days' notice.

**11.6 Relationship.** Independent contractors. No employment, agency, or partnership.

---

## Amendment No. 1 — DPCA-2024-0018-A1

**Effective date:** 2024-09-01

**A1.1 Payment Terms.** Section 3.3 amended: Client payment period extended from twenty-one
(21) to **thirty (30) calendar days** from the invoice date.

**A1.2 Late Payment Interest.** Section 3.4 amended: interest rate increased from 1.5% to
**2.0% per month** (or the maximum permitted by law, whichever is lower). This supersedes
the original rate.

**A1.3 Rate Adjustment Cap.** Section 3.6 amended: annual rate increase cap reduced from
seven percent (7%) to **five percent (5%)** per year, effective from the 2026-02-15 renewal.

**A1.4 Breach Cure Period.** Section 10.2 amended: the cure period for material breach is
extended from twenty-one (21) to **thirty (30) days** after written notice of breach.

**A1.5 Security Incident Notification.** Section 6.3 amended: Consultant must notify Client
within **twelve (12) hours** (reduced from twenty-four hours) of discovering any security
incident or personal data breach.

**A1.6 No Other Changes.** All other Agreement terms remain unchanged and in full force.

---

## Amendment No. 2 — DPCA-2024-0018-A2

**Effective date:** 2025-03-01

**A2.1 Confidentiality Duration.** Section 5.3 amended: post-termination confidentiality
period extended from six (6) to **eight (8) years**. Trade secret protection remains indefinite.

**A2.2 Data Deletion Timeline.** Section 6.5 amended: data deletion period shortened from
fifteen (15) to **ten (10) business days** post-termination, with written certification due
within five (5) business days of completion.

**A2.3 Liability Cap Revised.** Section 8.2 amended: the aggregate liability cap is increased
from six (6) months' fees to **twelve (12) months' fees** paid before the triggering event.

**A2.4 New Section 12 — Audit Rights.**

> "**12.1** Client may, not more than once per calendar year on twenty-one (21) days' written
> notice, audit Consultant's records relevant to fee calculations, data processing, and security
> compliance. Consultant shall cooperate and provide reasonable access. Client bears costs
> unless the audit reveals a discrepancy exceeding five percent (5%) in Consultant's favour,
> in which case Consultant bears reasonable audit costs."

**A2.5 No Other Changes.** All prior Agreement and Amendment No. 1 terms remain in full force.
