# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of 2021-01-14.

## Section 1: Term
The contract term shall be 2 years.

## Section 3: Payment Terms
Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.0% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## Section 5: Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
