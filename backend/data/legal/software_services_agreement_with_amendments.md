**Corpus note:** Sample for RAG demonstration only. Not an executed instrument.

## Parties
- **Client:** Meridian Analytics Pvt. Ltd., 14 Whitefield Tech Park, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560 066.
- **Provider:** NovaSoft Solutions LLP, Tower B, Level 9, Cyber Gateway, Hyderabad, Telangana 500 081.

---

## Section 1 — Scope of Services

**1.1 General.** Provider shall perform software development, integration, maintenance, and
support services described in each executed Statement of Work (SOW). Each SOW is incorporated
into this Agreement. In case of conflict, the SOW prevails for its subject matter.

**1.2 Initial SOW.** SOW-001 (Schedule A) covers Phase 1: data ingestion pipelines, analytics
engine, and a web-based dashboard for Client's internal business intelligence platform.

**1.3 Change Orders.** Scope, timeline, or fee changes require a written change order signed
by both Parties before Provider is obligated to perform additional work.

**1.4 Subcontracting.** Provider may subcontract with prior written notice to Client where
the subcontractor will access Client Confidential Information. Provider remains fully liable.

---

## Section 2 — Term

**2.1 Initial Term.** 2023-03-01 to 2026-02-28 (three years), unless earlier terminated.

**2.2 Renewal.** Auto-renews annually unless either Party gives ninety (90) days' written
notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Term.

---

## Section 3 — Fees and Payment

**3.1 Fees.** As specified in each SOW, denominated in INR unless stated otherwise.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Provider invoices monthly for the preceding month, or at SOW-specified intervals.

**3.3 Payment Terms.** Client pays each invoice within thirty (30) calendar days of the
invoice date (the "Payment Due Date").

**3.4 Late Payment Interest.** Undisputed overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.25% per month
(or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower) from the Payment Due Date until paid.

**3.5 Disputed Invoices.** Client pays undisputed amounts by the due date, notifies Provider
of disputes within fifteen (15) days with reasons, and both Parties resolve in thirty (30) days.
Late interest does not accrue on amounts under timely, bona fide dispute.

**3.6 Fee Adjustments.** Provider may adjust rates at each Renewal Term on sixty (60) days'
notice. Increases shall not exceed ten percent (10%) per Renewal Term.

---

## Section 4 — Intellectual Property

**4.1 Background IP.** Each Party retains its pre-existing IP. No transfer except as stated below.

**4.2 Client-Owned Deliverables.** Upon full payment, custom software and documentation
identified as "Client IP" in a SOW becomes Client's exclusive property. Provider assigns all
associated IP rights worldwide.

**4.3 Provider Retained IP.** Provider retains general methodologies, tools, and generic
components not specific to Client's business.

---

## Section 5 — Confidentiality

**5.1 Obligations.** Each Party holds the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence,
uses it only for this Agreement, and does not disclose it without prior written consent.

**5.2 Duration.** Five (5) years post-termination. Trade secrets: indefinite.

**5.3 Return/Destruction.** On termination or request, the Receiving Party returns or destroys
all Confidential Information materials and certifies compliance in writing.

---

## Section 6 — Data Protection

**6.1 Processing.** Provider processes Personal Data only on Client's documented instructions.

**6.2 Security.** Provider implements appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

**6.3 Breach Notification.** Provider notifies Client within seventy-two (72) hours of discovering
a personal data breach.

**6.4 Deletion.** On termination, Provider deletes or returns all Personal Data within
thirty (30) days and certifies completion in writing.

---

## Section 7 — Liability

**7.1 No Indirect Damages.** Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive
damages including lost profits or lost data.

**7.2 Aggregate Cap.** Total liability of either Party for all claims shall not exceed twelve
(12) months' fees paid or payable by Client before the event giving rise to the claim.

**7.3 Exceptions.** Cap does not apply to: wilful misconduct, gross negligence, fraud, death
or personal injury from negligence, Client's payment obligations, or Provider's indemnities.

---

## Section 8 — Termination

**8.1 For Convenience.** Either Party may terminate on ninety (90) days' written notice.

**8.2 For Cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches
and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

**8.3 Termination Fee.** If Client terminates for convenience or Provider terminates for cause,
Client pays a fee equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of remaining active SOW fees. No fee if
Client terminates for cause or Provider terminates for convenience.

---

## Section 9 — Dispute Resolution

**9.1 Escalation.** Management-level good-faith negotiation within thirty (30) days of notice.

**9.2 Arbitration.** Unresolved disputes go to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act 1996 (India), seat Bengaluru, Karnataka, English language, sole arbitrator.

**9.3 Governing Law.** Laws of India.

## Amendment No. 2 — SSA-2023-0047-A2

**Effective date:** 2024-01-10

**A2.1 Payment Terms.** Section 3.3 amended: general payment terms extended to **forty-five
(45) calendar days**. SOW-002 milestone invoices remain thirty (30) calendar days.

**A2.2 Late Payment Rate.** Section 3.4 amended: rate increased from 1.25% to **1.75% per
month**. This supersedes the original rate.

**A2.3 Termination Notice.** Section 8.1 amended: convenience termination notice increased
from ninety (90) to **one hundred and twenty (120) days**.

**A2.4 Audit Rights.** New Section 10 added:

> "Subscriber may audit Provider's records once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice.
> Provider cooperates. Client bears costs unless discrepancy exceeds 5% in Provider's favour."

**A2.5 No Other Changes.** All prior Agreement and Amendment No. 1 terms remain in full force.

---

## Amendment No. 3 — SSA-2023-0047-A3

**Effective date:** 2024-08-01

**A3.1 Confidentiality Duration.** Section 5.2 amended: post-termination period extended
from five (5) to **seven (7) years**. Trade secrets remain indefinite.

**A3.2 Information Security.** New Section 5.4 added: Provider shall maintain ISO/IEC 27001:2022
(or equivalent), conduct annual risk assessments, notify Client within **forty-eight (48) hours**
of a security incident, and test BCP/DR plans annually.

**A3.3 Fee Adjustment Cap.** Section 3.6 amended: annual increase cap reduced from ten percent
(10%) to **eight percent (8%)** per Renewal Term, effective from the first renewal after 2024-08-01.

**A3.4 SLA Commitment.** New Section 11 added for SOW-003 maintenance period: Platform
availability target of **99.5% per month**. Scheduled maintenance windows: Sundays 01:00–05:00
IST with seventy-two (72) hours' advance notice.

**A3.5 SLA Credits.**

| Monthly Availability | Credit |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.49% | 5% of monthly fee |
| 98.0% – 98.99% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Below 98.0% | 20% of monthly fee |

Credits capped at 50% of any single month's fee and are Client's sole remedy for SLA failures.

**A3.6 No Other Changes.** All prior Agreement terms remain unchanged and in full force.
