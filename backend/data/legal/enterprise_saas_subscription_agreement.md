# Enterprise SaaS Subscription Agreement — ESSA-2022-0031

**Document type:** Contract with amendments | **Effective date:** 2022-06-01
**Corpus note:** Sample for RAG demonstration only. Not an executed instrument.

## Parties
- **Subscriber:** PrimeRetail Ventures Pvt. Ltd., 8th Floor, Nexus Tower, MG Road, Pune, Maharashtra 411 001.
- **Vendor:** CloudAxis Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Plot 42, Electronic City Phase II, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560 100.

---

## Section 1 — Subscription Licence

**1.1 Grant.** Vendor grants Subscriber a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the
Subscription Term to access and use the cloud-based AxisERP SaaS Platform for internal
business operations, for up to the Authorised User count in the applicable Order Form.

**1.2 Restrictions.** Subscriber shall not reverse-engineer, sublicense, resell, or use the
Platform to process data on behalf of third parties. Subscriber is responsible for all
activity under Authorised User accounts.

**1.3 Vendor Reserved Rights.** All IP rights in the Platform remain with Vendor. No rights
are granted except the limited licence above.

---

## Section 2 — Subscription Term

**2.1 Initial Term.** Commences 2022-06-01 and expires 2025-05-31 (three years).

**2.2 Renewal.** Auto-renews for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives sixty
(60) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current Term.

---

## Section 3 — Fees and Payment

**3.1 Subscription Fees.** As set out in the Order Form, billed annually in advance.

**3.2 Payment Terms.** Invoices are due within thirty (30) calendar days of the invoice date.

**3.3 Late Payment Interest.** Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month,
or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower, until paid in full.

**3.4 Price Adjustments.** Vendor may increase fees at renewal on forty-five (45) days' notice,
not exceeding eight percent (8%) per renewal year.

**3.5 Suspension.** If any undisputed invoice is more than thirty (30) days overdue, Vendor
may suspend access on seven (7) days' written notice and restore it upon full payment.

---

## Section 4 — Availability and Support

**4.1 Uptime Commitment.** Vendor targets 99.5% monthly Uptime excluding scheduled maintenance.

**4.2 Maintenance Windows.** Saturdays 23:00–03:00 IST Sunday; forty-eight (48) hours' notice.

**4.3 SLA Credits.** For Uptime below 99.5% in any calendar month:

| Monthly Uptime | Credit (% of that month's fee) |
|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.49% | 5% |
| 98.0% – 98.99% | 10% |
| Below 98.0% | 20% |

Credits are Subscriber's sole remedy for Uptime failures and shall not exceed 50% of one
month's fee.

---

## Section 5 — Subscriber Data

**5.1 Ownership.** Subscriber retains all rights in Subscriber Data. Vendor processes it
only to provide the Platform.

**5.2 Portability.** Subscriber may export data at any time via the Platform's export tools.
After termination, Vendor makes data available for thirty (30) days then deletes it.

**5.3 Data Protection.** Vendor processes Personal Data on Subscriber's instructions only,
implements appropriate security measures, notifies Subscriber within forty-eight (48) hours
of a confirmed data breach, and deletes all Personal Data within thirty (30) days of expiry.

---

## Section 6 — Confidentiality

**6.1 Obligations.** Each Party holds the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence
and uses it only for the purposes of this Agreement. Obligations survive for five (5) years
post-termination and indefinitely for trade secrets.

---

## Section 7 — Liability and Indemnification

**7.1 No Indirect Damages.** Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential
damages including lost profits or lost data.

**7.2 Aggregate Cap.** Total liability of either Party for all claims shall not exceed twelve
(12) months' Subscription Fees paid before the event giving rise to the claim.

**7.3 Vendor Indemnification.** Vendor indemnifies Subscriber against third-party IP
infringement claims arising from the Platform used in accordance with this Agreement.

---

## Section 8 — Termination

**8.1 For Convenience.** Either Party may terminate on sixty (60) days' written notice.
Pre-paid fees are non-refundable unless Subscriber terminates for Vendor's material breach.

**8.2 For Cause.** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party materially
breaches and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice, or becomes insolvent.

**8.3 Refund on Subscriber Cause Termination.** If Subscriber terminates for Vendor's
material breach, Vendor refunds pre-paid unused fees pro-rata from the termination date.

---

## Section 9 — General

**9.1 Governing Law.** Laws of India. Arbitration seat: Pune, Maharashtra.

**9.2 Notices.** Written; delivered by registered post or email with read-receipt.

**9.3 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and all Order Forms are the entire agreement.

**9.4 Force Majeure.** No liability for delays beyond reasonable control. If event exceeds
forty-five (45) days, either Party may terminate on fifteen (15) days' notice without liability.

---

## Amendment No. 1 — ESSA-2022-0031-A1

**Effective date:** 2023-08-01

**A1.1 Late Payment Interest Rate.** Section 3.3 is deleted and replaced:

> **"3.3 Late Payment Interest.** Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at **1.5% per
> month** (or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower). This replaces the
> original rate of 1.0% per month."

**A1.2 Payment Terms Extended.** Section 3.2 is amended: payment terms are changed from
thirty (30) to **forty-five (45) calendar days** from the invoice date.

**A1.3 Uptime Target Upgraded.** Section 4.1 is amended: the monthly Uptime commitment
is increased from 99.5% to **99.8%**.

**A1.4 Confidentiality Duration.** Section 6.1 is amended: the post-termination
confidentiality period is extended from five (5) years to **seven (7) years**.

**A1.5 No Other Changes.** All other Agreement terms remain unchanged.

---

## Amendment No. 2 — ESSA-2022-0031-A2

**Effective date:** 2024-04-01

**A2.1 Price Adjustment Cap.** Section 3.4 is amended: the annual fee increase cap is
reduced from eight percent (8%) to **six percent (6%)** per renewal year, effective from
the 2025-06-01 renewal onwards.

**A2.2 Data Breach Notification Window.** Section 5.3 is amended: the data breach
notification period is reduced from forty-eight (48) hours to **twenty-four (24) hours**.

**A2.3 New Section 10 — Audit Rights.** A new provision is added:

> **"10.1 Audit Rights.** Subscriber may, once per calendar year on thirty (30) days'
> written notice, audit Vendor's records relevant to fee calculation and data processing
> compliance. Vendor shall cooperate and grant reasonable access. Subscriber bears audit
> costs unless the audit reveals a discrepancy exceeding five percent (5%) in Vendor's
> favour, in which case Vendor bears reasonable audit costs."

**A2.4 No Other Changes.** All other Agreement and Amendment No. 1 terms remain unchanged.
