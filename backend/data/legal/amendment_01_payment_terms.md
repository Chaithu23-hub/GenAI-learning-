# Amendment No. 1 to Master Services Agreement

**Amendment reference:** MSA-AMEND-001  
**Effective date:** 2021-03-15  
**Amends:** Master Services Agreement — MSA-2021-001 (effective date 2021-01-14)  
**Corpus note:** Sample amendment for RAG demonstration only. Not an executed instrument.

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into to amend the Master Services Agreement.

## Section 3: Payment Terms
Section 3 of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:
Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## Changes made by this amendment
This Amendment No. 1 modifies the Master Services Agreement by replacing Section 3: late payments now accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month instead of the original 1.0% per month. All other terms and conditions of the Master Services Agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect.
