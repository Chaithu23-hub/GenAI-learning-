## Part 2 — Breach Notification Deadlines

For the purposes of **Section 7.6** of the Agreement (inserted by Amendment No. 2, Section 3), a Party intending to terminate for cause must give notice of the material breach within the following period after becoming aware of it:

| Breach Category | Notice Deadline |
|---|---|
| Payment breach | 30 calendar days |
| Service Level breach | **15 Business Days** |
| Confidentiality breach | 10 Business Days |
| Data protection breach | 10 Business Days |
| All other material breaches | 30 calendar days |

Failure to give notice within the applicable period waives the right to terminate in respect of that breach.

## Part 3 — Combined Cure and Notice Sequence

Where a Party terminates for cause following a Service Level breach, the full sequence is:

1. Notice of material breach must be given within **15 Business Days** of awareness (Schedule B-2, Part 2).
2. The breaching Party then has the **Cure Period** to remedy — **45 days** as amended by Amendment No. 1, Section 4.
3. If unremedied at the end of the Cure Period, the non-breaching Party may terminate with immediate effect under Section 7.4(a).
