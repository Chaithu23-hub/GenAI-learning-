---

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Effective Date set out above by and between **Soft Suave Technologies Private Limited** ("**Provider**") and **Vertex Retail Solutions Limited**, a company incorporated under the laws of Singapore with its registered office at 1 Raffles Place, Singapore ("**Client**").

---

## Section 1. Definitions

**1.1 "Affiliate"** has the meaning given in Section 1.1 of the standard Provider terms: any entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with a Party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of voting securities.

**1.2 "Business Day"** means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a public holiday observed in **Singapore**. This Agreement does not incorporate a separate holiday calendar schedule.

**1.3 "Confidential Information"** means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

**1.4 "Cure Period"** means a period of **twenty (20) days** from the date a Party receives written notice of a material breach, within which that Party may remedy the breach.

**1.5 "Deliverable"** means any work product, document, software, report, or other material that Provider is required to deliver to Client under a Statement of Work.

**1.6 "Services"** means the services described in Schedule A to this Agreement.

---

## Section 2. Scope of Services

**2.1** Provider shall perform the Services described in Schedule A in accordance with the Service Levels set out in Schedule B to this Agreement.

**2.2** In the event of a conflict between a Statement of Work and this Agreement, this Agreement prevails.

---

## Section 3. Fees and Payment

**3.1 Fees.** Client shall pay Provider the fees set out in Schedule B to this Agreement. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Client monthly in arrears.

**3.3 Payment Terms.** Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within **sixty (60) days** of the invoice date.

**3.4 Late Payment.** Any undisputed amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at a rate of **one percent (1.0%) per month**, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower.

**3.5 Disputed Amounts.** Client may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, provided Client notifies Provider within ten (10) Business Days of the invoice date.

**3.6 Fee Increases.** Provider may increase fees by not more than **three percent (3%)** per annum on each anniversary of the Effective Date, on not less than ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

---

## Section 5. Confidentiality

**5.1** Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in confidence and shall use a **reasonable degree of care** to prevent its unauthorised disclosure.

**5.2** Neither Party shall disclose the other Party's Confidential Information except to employees, Affiliates, and professional advisers who have a need to know.

**5.3 Survival.** The obligations in this Section 5 shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years**.

---

## Section 6. Intellectual Property

**6.1** Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property.

**6.2** Subject to payment of all amounts due, Provider assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables, excluding Provider pre-existing intellectual property.

---

## Section 7. Term and Termination

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of **three (3) years**, expiring on **2 March 2029**, unless terminated earlier.

**7.2 Renewal.** This Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of one (1) year unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

**7.3 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party not less than **ninety (90) days'** prior written notice, provided that no such notice may be given during the first twenty-four (24) months of the initial term.

**7.4 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate with immediate effect if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within the Cure Period, becomes insolvent, or ceases to carry on business.

---

## Section 8. Limitation of Liability

**8.1 Exclusion of Indirect Loss.** Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, revenue, business, or anticipated savings.

**8.2 Direct Loss.** Each Party is liable for direct losses arising from its breach, subject to the cap in Section 8.3.

**8.3 Liability Cap.** The aggregate liability of each Party under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed **five hundred thousand United States dollars (USD 500,000)** in aggregate over the life of this Agreement.

**8.4 Exclusions from Cap.** The limitations in Sections 8.1 and 8.3 do not apply to: (a) indemnity obligations under Section 9; (b) breach of Section 5; (c) fraud or wilful misconduct; or (d) Client's obligation to pay fees properly due.

---

## Section 9. Indemnities

**9.1** Provider shall indemnify Client against third-party claims that a Deliverable infringes third-party intellectual property rights, subject to prompt notice, sole control of defence, and reasonable assistance.

**9.2** Client shall indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client's unlawful use of the Deliverables.

---

## Section 10. Data Protection

**10.1** Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection laws, including the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

**10.2** Provider shall process Client personal data only in accordance with Client's documented instructions.

**10.3 Breach Notification.** Provider shall notify Client of any personal data breach within **forty-eight (48) hours** of becoming aware of it.

---

## Section 11. Audit

**11.1** Client may audit Provider's compliance **once per calendar year**, on not less than **thirty (30) days'** prior written notice.

---

## Section 13. Insurance

**13.1** Provider shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than **USD 2,000,000** per claim and commercial general liability insurance of not less than **USD 5,000,000** per occurrence.

---

## Section 14. Notices

**14.1** Any notice shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by internationally recognised courier, or by electronic mail to the addresses set out in the signature block below.

**14.2** A notice sent by courier is deemed received on the **third** Business Day after dispatch.

---

## Section 15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**15.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of **Singapore**.

**15.2 Arbitration.** Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by **arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC)** in accordance with its Rules in force at the time. The seat of arbitration shall be Singapore and the tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator.

**15.3** For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement does **not** confer jurisdiction on any court in India.

---

## Section 17. Non-Solicitation

**17.1** During the term and for **six (6) months** thereafter, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party materially involved in the performance of this Agreement.

---

## Section 20. Amendment

**20.1** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. **As at the date of this document, no amendments to this Agreement have been executed.**
