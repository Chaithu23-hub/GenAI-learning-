# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Amendment No.:** AMD-2026-014-01
**Amends:** MSA-2026-014 (Master Services Agreement)
**Counterparty:** Northwind Logistics Private Limited
**Provider:** Soft Suave Technologies Private Limited
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2026
**Document Version:** v1.1
**Status:** Executed — superseded in part by Amendment No. 2

---

This Amendment No. 1 (this "**Amendment**") is made as of 1 April 2026 between **Soft Suave Technologies Private Limited** ("Provider") and **Northwind Logistics Private Limited** ("Client"), and amends the Master Services Agreement No. MSA-2026-014 dated 14 January 2026 (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## Section 1. New Defined Terms

**1.1** Section 1 of the Agreement is amended by inserting the following new definitions:

**"Critical Service"** means any Service designated as Critical in Schedule A, Part 2.

**"Escalation Contact"** means the individual named for each escalation tier in **Schedule B-2 (Notice and Escalation Matrix)**.

**"Priority Incident"** means an incident affecting a Critical Service that prevents Client from processing shipments, for which the response and resolution targets in Schedule B, Part 2 apply.

---

## Section 2. Payment Terms

**2.1** Section 3.3 (Payment Terms) of the Agreement is **deleted and replaced** with the following:

> "**3.3 Payment Terms.** Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within **forty-five (45) days** of the invoice date."

**2.2** For the avoidance of doubt, the late payment interest rate in Section 3.4 of the Agreement is unchanged and remains at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month.

---

## Section 3. Termination for Convenience

**3.1** Section 7.3 (Termination for Convenience) of the Agreement is **deleted and replaced** with the following:

> "**7.3 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party not less than **thirty (30) days'** prior written notice, provided that no such notice may be given during the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term."

---

## Section 4. Cure Period

**4.1** Section 1.4 (Cure Period) of the Agreement is **deleted and replaced** with the following:

> "**1.4 'Cure Period'** means a period of **forty-five (45) days** from the date a Party receives written notice of a material breach, within which that Party may remedy the breach."

**4.2** For the avoidance of doubt, where the Agreement provides that a Cure Period does not apply — including upon a Qualifying Event under Section 4.3 of the Agreement — that provision is unchanged.

---

## Section 5. Service Credits

**5.1** Schedule B, Part 3 (Service Credits) of the Agreement is amended by increasing the maximum monthly service credit from five percent (5%) to **ten percent (10%)** of the monthly fees for the affected Service.

---

## Section 6. Audit Frequency

**6.1** Section 11.1 of the Agreement is amended by replacing "once per calendar year" with "**twice per calendar year**", provided that the second audit in any calendar year shall be at Client's sole cost regardless of its findings.

---

## Section 7. No Other Changes

**7.1** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

**7.2** In the event of a conflict between this Amendment and the Agreement, this Amendment governs in respect of the provisions it amends.
